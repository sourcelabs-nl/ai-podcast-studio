A candidate device observed in [[dutch-two-host-shows]] and not yet tried here.

One story of the episode is not closed when its segment ends. It is returned to
as later stories touch it, so it accumulates rather than repeats, and the
episode has a spine the listener can follow instead of a list of items. It
differs from the existing MID-ROLL CALLBACKS rule, which asks for a reference
back; an anchor asks for one story to keep developing.

It is not in the prompt and has no OpenSpec change. Attempting it means deciding
how the anchor is chosen, since the model already front-loads the best story and
the anchor is likely the same one.
