Two Dutch two-host news podcasts were transcribed and read as reference for what
a conversation between two people sounds like when it holds attention. The
transcripts are raw material and are not reproduced here.

What transfers:

- Humor is reactive and shared. Neither host arrives with a joke; the laughs
  land on what the other just said, and both produce them. This is the basis of
  [[humor-is-not-one-speakers-job]].
- An anchor story is reused as a throughline rather than closed and left behind.
  See [[anchor-story-device]].

What does not transfer:

- Their register is conflict-free. The hosts agree, elaborate and enjoy each
  other, and almost never push back. Copying that here would remove the friction
  the interviewer rule deliberately asks for, since our interviewer is an
  audience surrogate who is meant to challenge and disagree.
